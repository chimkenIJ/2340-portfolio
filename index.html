Web App!

Features & Functionality

The GT Movie Store web app allows users to find & review movies, see what others think of it, and buy them!

Firstly, the user can make an account to later purchase movies, and leave reviews for others to see. There's an about page to get them situated on the navbar, explaining the website. Next, they can see the list of movies currently offered by the store. They can also search for specific ones, if they already know what they're looking for. After seeing a movie they like, the user can click into it, to see the description, price, and reviews left by other users. If they're logged in, they can also leave (editable) reviews! There's also an option to report other reviews, removing it from the user's view, leaving it up to the admin to delete it from the website. Next, they can add the movie to their cart after choosing the quantity. This automatically redirects them to the cart, where they can either purchase all the movies in the cart, or delete them. Finally, once the user orders their moveis, they can see their past orders in the orders tab. There's also an option to logout on the top right, in case they need to switch accounts. In addition, anyone can access it online! I hosted it on pythonanywhere here!
